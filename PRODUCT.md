# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Rohan uses Toby on his Mac as a personal second brain: thinking aloud, capturing meetings, writing notes and continuing practical work. It is not primarily a coding tool. He performs visual and functionality QA himself.

## Product Purpose

Make speaking a natural way into a durable personal workspace. Toby always replies in text. Conversations, meeting recordings, notes and generated work belong in the main app. Settings live in an in-app side drawer. A new voice conversation opens a new thread page, not a separate window.

## Brand Personality

Personal, warm, considered. Preserve continuity with the original Toby identity and icon, while removing the bland corporate feel of the first rewrite.

## Anti-references

Generic chatbot sidebars, corporate dashboards, robotic typography, identical cards everywhere, separate settings/recording windows, spoken assistant replies.

## Design Principles

- Start from a thought, not a control panel.
- Keep capture, context and the resulting text together.
- Give each artifact an appropriate place and presentation.
- Use motion to explain entering voice mode, never to delay interaction.
- Keep active microphones and recording controls visible when navigating.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native keyboard access, meaningful labels, selectable text and system permissions. Honor Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency. Do not rely on color alone for recording state. No agent-driven visual or device QA.
